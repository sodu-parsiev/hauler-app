Enable Hauler in Safari

Grant marketplace access

Hauler needs access to Taobao, Tmall, Weidian, 1688, CnFans, Acbuy, Kakobuy, and Oopbuy to open listings in the app.

  1. Open Safari.
  2. Tap aA → Manage Extensions.
  3. Turn on Hauler Helper.
  4. Select Allow on All Websites (or specific sites).
